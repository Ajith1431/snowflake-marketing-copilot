5. Creative Direction

Grounded in Samsung brand guidelines — voice is innovative, trustworthy, intelligent, speaking as a trusted advisor, benefit-led not feature-led, and never disparaging competitors directly (differentiate on data-backed strengths).

  • Messaging themes: (1) "The whole upgrade, one deal" — the full Galaxy ecosystem (phone + Book + TV + watch) in a single Black Friday basket; (2) "Real savings, bought direct" — confident, data-backed value claims (legal-approved) rather than comparative attacks; (3) "Smart buyers buy today" — event-day urgency.
  • Visuals: primary palette #0077B6 / #00B4D8 / #023E8A / #CAF0F8; real people in authentic settings (no staged stock); modern sans-serif headlines, clean serif body; WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all assets.
  • Hierarchy: Brand Promise → Category → Product → a single measurable CTA ("Shop the Black Friday deal") per asset; A/B test all headlines before scaling.
