1. Executive Summary

This pitch outlines a $200,000 Brand Awareness campaign for Nike's Running Collection, built on NovaSpark's pooled historical performance data and Nike's approved brand guidelines. The plan spreads budget evenly across four high-reach digital channels — TikTok, Facebook, Programmatic Display, and Google Search — selected and ranked by their historical impressions-per-dollar efficiency.

At full flight, the campaign is projected to deliver 26.5M impressions, 748K clicks, 34,508 conversions, and $481,862 in attributed revenue, for a blended 2.41x ROAS at a $0.27 blended CPC. Because the objective is awareness, impressions and reach are the primary success measures; conversions and revenue are secondary efficiency indicators. The campaign is well-timed to launch into the New Year 2027 fitness-resolution season, when consumer spend on fitness, health, and self-improvement peaks.

6. Creative Direction

Grounded in Nike's approved brand guidelines:

  • Voice & tone: Bold, elegant, expressive — speaking to the audience as a trusted advisor, empowering readers to take confident action. Tone flexes more casual on TikTok/Facebook and more authoritative in longer-form placements, while holding the core personality.
  • Messaging hierarchy: Lead with the brand promise and category story, then the Running Collection, then a discovery-stage CTA — benefits before features. A/B test all headlines before scaling.
  • Visual identity: Primary palette of black (#000000), white (#FFFFFF), gold (#C9B037), and taupe (#8B7355). Photography must feature real runners in authentic settings — never staged or corporate stock. Maintain logomark clear space and meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
  • Competitive posture: Never disparage competitors directly; differentiate on Nike's own innovation and reliability, backed by data.
Seasonal creative hook: Align launch creative to the New Year 2027 resolution moment, when fitness and self-improvement spending surges — a natural awareness window for a running product. Valentine's Day 2027 offers a secondary apparel-gifting beat to extend the flight.
