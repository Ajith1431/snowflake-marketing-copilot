Strategy Rationale (grounded in historical data)

Why these channels for awareness. The plan ranks channels by historical impressions-per-dollar, the right lens for an awareness objective. The product's own channel history reinforces three of the four picks:
  • TikTok — 2.55x ROAS on Running Collection history (5 product campaigns) and 17 campaigns behind the pooled rate; the strongest social performer for reaching the younger, discovery-driven cohort.
  • Facebook — 2.37x ROAS on product history (5 campaigns), 18 campaigns behind the rate — the deepest evidence base of any channel here, which supports confidence in its reach efficiency.
  • Google Search — 4.30x ROAS on Running Collection history, albeit on a thin base of 2 product campaigns; it anchors high-intent demand capture at a higher CPC ($0.38). The 10 campaigns behind the pooled rate temper confidence somewhat.
  • Programmatic Display — lowest cost-per-click ($0.22) in the plan and the highest projected conversions (10,235), making it an efficient reach-extender (3 product campaigns historically; 10 behind the pooled rate).
Creative direction. Nike's voice is bold, elegant, and expressive, speaking to the audience as trusted advisors and always empowering the reader to take confident action; tone should flex more casual on social (TikTok/Facebook) and more authoritative in search. Follow the messaging hierarchy — Brand Promise → Category → Product → Call to Action — leading with benefits over features and including a clear, funnel-appropriate CTA; A/B test headlines before scaling. Any competitive claims must be data-substantiated and legal-approved, and never disparage rivals directly. Market timing. The campaign sits ahead of a dense Q4 retail window — Singles Day (Nov 11), Black Friday (Nov 27), Cyber Monday (Nov 30), and the Holiday Season (Dec 1) — all strong moments to amplify an awareness push into high-traffic shopping periods. Looking further out, the FIFA Women's World Cup 2027 (starts Jun 24, 2027) is a marquee upcoming moment for women's-sports marketing and sponsorship activation — a natural tentpole for a running-focused brand to plan a follow-on flight around, especially given the female-leaning segments above.
