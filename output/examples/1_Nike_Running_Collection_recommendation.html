Target Audience Segments

Nike's brand guidance requires each brief to name at least two personas and to let data drive persona refinement rather than assumptions. Nike's primary audience is decision-makers aged 25–54 who value quality and innovation, with a secondary digital-native audience aged 18–24 who discover brands on social platforms. Mapping that to Nike's registered segments, the recommended targeting for the Running Collection is:

  • Athleisure Fans (22–39, balanced gender, medium income; ~1.44M reach) — core running/active lifestyle buyers; anchor segment for TikTok and Facebook social reach.
  • Occasion Shoppers (25–44, female-leaning, medium-high income; ~1.67M reach) — Nike's largest addressable segment; strong fit for Programmatic Display prospecting.
  • Luxury Fashion Buyers (25–49, female-leaning, high income; ~1.53M reach) — premium runners/athleisure crossover; aligns with Nike's quality-and-innovation positioning on Google Search intent.
  • Streetwear Enthusiasts (18–29, balanced; ~196K reach) — the digital-native 18–24 secondary audience, best activated on TikTok.

Lead with Athleisure Fans + Occasion Shoppers as the two required primary personas, with Streetwear Enthusiasts as a TikTok-specific youth layer.
